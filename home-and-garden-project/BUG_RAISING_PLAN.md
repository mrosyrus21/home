---

## Governing rule

**Predators never share an enclosure with prey.** Everything below follows from this.

**Minimum hardware: 3 containers**
1. Herbivore display enclosure (Goal 1)
2. Solo jumping-spider enclosure (Goal 2)
3. One disposable deli-cup aphid/ladybug culture (Goal 3)

---

## Goal 1 — Keep & raise interesting plant-pest bugs as pets

One **display enclosure** of herbivores:
- **Katydids** (have them now) — moved off the basil so the basil can recover.
- **Cleanup crew:** isopods + springtails.
- **Open to adding** more herbivores found in the wild — e.g. a **stick insect** as a compatible roommate.
- **Size-matching rule:** only house bugs of similar size together. A bigger bug will nibble a smaller one mid-molt, when it's soft and defenseless.

All herbivores, one enclosure. This is the flexible "whatever cool herbivore turns up" tank.

## Goal 2 — Raise the jumping spider (SOLO)

- **Always solo** — it's a predator, so it never shares with prey.
- **Food:** found "throwaway" pests not worth keeping + **flightless fruit flies** + eventually the **auto fly-feeder** contraption (see the site's Tank tab side project).
- **NO CRICKETS.** Cyrus does not want to feed crickets. Spider food is flightless fruit flies and found bad bugs instead.

## Goal 3 — Raise good bugs (ladybugs) FOR THE GARDEN

- Ladybugs are **predators too**, so they live separately from the herbivores.
- **The clever part:** ladybugs eat aphids, so the **aphid culture and the ladybug nursery are the SAME cheap container** — a disposable deli cup with an aphid-covered plant cutting + ladybug larvae.
- Grown ladybugs get **released onto the garden plants** to eat aphids.
- That same culture **also feeds the jumping spider.**

---

## The food web

```
Aphid / ladybug deli-cup culture ──┬──> feeds the jumping spider
                                   └──> grows ladybugs ──> released onto garden plants (eat aphids)

Cleanup crew (isopods + springtails) ──> maintains the herbivore display

Katydids ──> moved OFF the basil ──> basil recovers
```

---

## Why "flexible"

Cyrus is sourcing a lot of this from **wild finds**, so the exact species in each enclosure will shift over time. The plan locks in the *rules and roles* (predator/prey separation, size-matching, the 3 containers, the food web) and stays open on the *specific bugs*.

**Temporary now, bioactive later:** the current Petco run (see the site's Tank tab → "Bug Starter Kit · TEMPORARY") is a quick starter only. The full enclosures + bioactive ecosystem come later.
