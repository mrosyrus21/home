## ⚠️ Active Alerts

### Fungus Gnat Treatment (Indoor Plants)
- **Root cause:** Overwatering
- **Protocol:** Do NOT water until soil is bone dry
- **Treatment:** BTI tea (Mosquito Bits), yellow sticky traps
- **Rule:** Always empty saucers after every watering — never let water sit
- Pots have drainage rocks + weed barrier in place

### Greenhouse Watering Rule
- Water **mornings only** — never evenings (humidity + gnat risk)

### ⚠️ Peppermint Decline (May 29)
- Peppermint has died back to mostly bare soil + sparse stems since May 14
- **Check the crown/roots:** firm & green at base → cut back dead growth, water mornings, expect regrowth (mint is resilient). Mushy base → root rot from overwatering
- Hold off watering until soil is dry

### Peppermint
- **Photo:** images/20260529_130641.jpg
- **Status (May 29):** ⚠️ Big decline — mostly bare soil with only sparse, thin purple stems left and lots of dried foliage. Has died back significantly since May 14
- **Care:** Check the roots/crown — if stems are still firm and green at the base, cut back the dead growth, move to morning water, and it may regenerate (mint is hard to kill). If crown is mushy, likely root rot from earlier overwatering
- **Note:** This is the one to watch. Don't water unless soil is dry; mint usually bounces back from the roots

- **Care:** Water sparingly (drought-tolerant); morning water only

### Dill
- **Photo:** images/20260529_130649.jpg
- **Status (May 29):** Tall, feathery, and healthy — mature and full
- **Care:** Morning water; will bolt (flower/seed) soon — harvest fronds now

## 🌿 Outdoor Plants
*Backyard — full sun exposure*

### Heirloom Tomato
- **Photos:** images/20260529_130515.jpg (full plant), 20260529_130534.jpg, 20260529_130540.jpg, 20260529_130526.jpg, 20260529_130531.jpg, 20260529_130605.jpg (fruit)
- **Status (May 29):** Big leap forward — large sprawling plant with several full-size green beefsteak fruit already set. Plant is heavy and leaning; some fruit show catfacing/zippering scars at the blossom end (the corky brown lines)
- **Care:** Water deeply and *consistently* every 2–3 days — irregular watering is what causes the catfacing/cracking. Re-stake or cage it; it's outgrowing the current bamboo support. Remove suckers and any leaves touching soil
- **Note:** Catfacing is cosmetic — fruit is still edible; just cut out the scar. Consistent moisture + a sturdier cage will clean up the next fruit
- **Reward:** Fresh heirloom tomatoes for summer cooking

### Husky Cherry Tomato
- **Photos:** images/20260529_130406.jpg (plant), 20260529_130341.jpg, 20260529_130400.jpg, 20260529_130621.jpg (fruit/flowers)
- **Status (May 29):** Compact and productive — yellow flowers plus small round green cherries already forming. Healthy
- **Care:** Water regularly; far less pruning than the heirloom. Keep it in good sun
- **Reward:** Snackable cherry tomatoes all summer

### Jalapeño
- **Photos:** images/20260529_130415.jpg, 20260529_130422.jpg
- **Status (May 29):** Improved — dark green, healthy leaves with strong new growth from the center. Past the slow-starter stage; not flowering yet
- **Care:** Full sun; water when the top inch of soil is dry. Feeding lightly now will push it toward flowering
- **Reward:** Fresh jalapeños for cooking and backyard BBQ

### Strawberry
- **Photos:** images/20260529_130455.jpg, 20260529_130508.jpg
- **Status (May 29):** Looking great — compact, deep green, white flowers open and at least one fruit beginning to form. The earlier edge-browning has cleared up
- **Care:** Keep moisture consistent; pinch a few early flowers if you want bigger berries later; pin runners if you want more plants
- **Note:** Much healthier than the last assessment — keep doing what you're doing
- **Reward:** Fresh strawberries through summer

### Raspberry
- **Photos:** images/20260529_130429.jpg, 20260529_130436.jpg, 20260529_130448.jpg
- **Status (May 29):** Robust and tall, staked with bamboo, large healthy leaves; only minor edge nibbling on a few leaves
- **Care:** Water deeply; keep it tied to the support as canes lengthen. First-year canes (primocanes) grow now, fruit next year
- **Reward:** Fresh raspberries (likely next season if first-year canes)

### Dianthus (Pinks)
- **Photo:** images/20260529_130321.jpg
- **Status (May 29):** Full, showy bloom — pink/white/magenta ruffled flowers, but a noticeable number of spent brown blooms mixed in
- **Care:** Deadhead the spent flowers now — it's the single best thing for continued blooming. Water at the base, morning
- **Note:** Beautiful color for the backyard hangout — just needs a deadheading pass

### Daisy
- **Photo:** images/20260529_130411.jpg
- **Status (May 29):** Blooming white/yellow daisies, but still showing stress — yellowing and browning on several lower leaves
- **Care:** Deadhead spent flowers; check that it isn't sitting in water (the nursery basket inside the terra cotta pot can trap moisture). Consider repotting into the terra cotta directly with drainage
- **Note:** Persistent leaf stress — most likely drainage/root crowding. Repotting is the fix

### Candytuft
- **Photo:** images/20260529_130331.jpg
- **Status (May 29):** Spring bloom is finishing — mostly green now with dried, seeding flower stalks and a leggy/sprawling shape
- **Care:** Shear it back by about a third now that flowering is done — this keeps it compact and can trigger a second flush. Minimal water
- **Note:** Right on schedule for a post-bloom trim
