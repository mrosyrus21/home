# My Nicotine Quit Plan

## Background
- Smoked cigarettes for 10 years, vaped for 5 years
- Currently at 1% nicotine vape
- Have herbal sticks (nicotine-free) available but unused
- Goal: Quit nicotine completely
- Approach: Gradual taper for long-lasting results
- Main triggers: Habit/hand-to-mouth, satisfying pull sensation, stress
- Have attempted quitting before

---

## The Strategy: Swap, Shrink, Stop

Since the primary triggers are habit and stress (not just nicotine dependency), the plan is to replace the ritual before dropping it entirely.

---

## Phase 1 — Swap (Weeks 1–2)
- Keep vaping, but limit to designated times only: morning, lunch, after work, evening (4x max)
- Any craving outside those times → use herbal stick instead
- Goal: Start breaking the reflex without white-knuckling

## Phase 2 — Shrink (Weeks 3–5)
- Cut to 2 vape sessions per day (morning and evening only)
- All other cravings → herbal stick
- Practice slow, deep inhales with the herbal stick so the body associates that pull satisfaction with something nicotine-free

## Phase 3 — Drop (Week 6)
- Stop vaping entirely
- Use herbal sticks freely — no guilt, they are the bridge
- Now nicotine-free, just managing the habit loop

## Phase 4 — Fade (Weeks 7–10)
- Gradually reduce herbal stick use by replacing with:
  - Cold water sips (for stress hits)
  - 4-7-8 breathing: inhale 4 seconds, hold 7 seconds, exhale 8 seconds (mimics the pull)
  - A fidget or toothpick for hand-to-mouth habit

---

## Handling Stress Triggers
Stress is the biggest risk factor. Before reaching for anything, apply the 60-second rule:
- Wait 60 seconds
- Do box breathing during that time
- The craving will often peak and drop within that window

---

## Mindset Note
This is not the first quit attempt, so the goal is progress, not perfection. A slip is not failure — it is data. Note what triggered it and adjust the plan accordingly.
